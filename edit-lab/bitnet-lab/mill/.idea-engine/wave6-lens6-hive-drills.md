### PL-001 ★ · Decay-triggered drill scheduling: practice fires on measured rot, not on the calendar
- **source:** Cycles 004 and 005 each birthed both targets with the battery flat at 0.941 → 0.941, then cycle 006 regressed it (0.941 → 0.934, sealed 0.8611 → 0.8056) roughly 6h later — nothing between cycles measures whether a member is *decaying*. The instrument already exists and sits idle between cycles: `factory/dream_state.json` holds thermostat probes for the bug classes (all three weak classes at edge band 1, `mastered: []`; off-by-one band-1 probe recall 0.0, wrong-operator 0.2222, swapped-operands 0.0), and `factory/dream.py` only runs them inside a dream cycle. HV-010 signal-gates *growth cycles* on evidence mass; no entry schedules *practice* on per-member decay.
- **mechanism:** A standing decay probe, separate from any cycle: on the daemon's cadence, each serving member/section is re-probed on fresh, never-trained draws (the thermostat's own probe machinery for bug classes; held/screen draws for classify niches), and the delta against that unit's last drill baseline is recorded. A unit whose probe recall or median margin falls ≥ 0.05 — or whose clean sentinel (PL-008) loses ≥ 1 dev item — is enqueued for a drill; units inside ±0.02 are left alone. Scheduling follows the rot, per unit, instead of the whole hive drilling (or never drilling) together.
- **change:** `factory/dream.py` — `Thermostat.measure()` / `_probe_band_accs()` gain a persisted decay record in `dream_state.json` (baseline, last probe, delta per class); `factory/daemon.py` — `FactoryDaemon.process_one()` gains the probe-and-enqueue step; `factory/scheduler.py` — `CycleQueue.enqueue()` accepts the drill request shape the daemon emits.
- **judged by:** Process bar first, labelled as such: over the next 8 cadences, zero drills enqueued for units with probe delta < 0.02, and every unit crossing the 0.05 line drilled within one cadence. Serving bar unchanged and drill-safe: probes and drills are dev/shadow only and spend no sealed measurement; any candidate a decay drill produces reaches serving only via PL-005's ladder ending at battery overall > 0.941, sealed ≥ 0.8611.
- **kill test:** Two consecutive battery or screen regressions in a unit whose decay probe stayed inside ±0.02 beforehand — the probe does not see the rot that matters; retire the trigger and record the probe as decoration, rather than scheduling on it.

### PL-003 · Cross-niche sparring: one niche's generator attacks another niche's judge
- **source:** Every generator in the factory only attacks its own niche: `factory/dream_gen_niches.py` `niche_generators()` registers exactly two subjects (contract-judgment, commit-type), and `factory/dream_gen.py` attacks only the bug composition. - **judged by:** Attack yield first: execution-proved disagreement rate per pair, reported per pair — a pair under 2% is a healthy boundary, not a failure to hide. Sparring writes nothing to serving directly.

### PL-004 · Drill budgets as a governed resource: a drill that cannot state its spend does not run
- **source:** Practice is not free and its costs are already on record: one dream cycle generated **628** verified items to keep **172** after contamination (456 dropped), and a growth cycle spends the sealed battery *twice* (before/after, HV-010's source). `factory/host.py`'s `Governor` governs load, heat, and heavy legs — nothing governs how much generation, training, or judging a practice regimen may consume, so a standing regimen (PL-001/007) without a budget is an unbounded background spend on a 1.5-CPU / 6 GB VM that runs one heavy job at a time.
- **mechanism:** A drill budget ledger with three currencies, charged per drill: generated items, training epochs, and *judge spends* — with the hard rule that drills charge **zero** judge spends: no drill may measure on sealed or battery items at all (those are spent only by PL-005's final promotion rung, once). A weekly cap per currency and a per-member cap weighted by measured failure mass (PL-001's decay deltas); a drill that hits its cap stops mid-regimen and reports partial, exactly as a governor backoff holds a cycle today. Marginal rule: when a member's last two drills each bought < 0.01 dev gain per 100 generated items, its cap halves until its decay delta re-earns it.
- **change:** `factory/host.py` — `GovernorConfig` / `Governor` gain the drill-budget decision; `factory/reporter.py` — `collect()` gains the drill-spend section (spend per drill, per member, per gain bought); `factory/scheduler.py` — `CycleQueue` drill requests carry their budget charge and are held, not popped, when the budget refuses.
- **judged by:** Process bars, labelled as such: 100% of drills in the ledger carry all three charges; zero drill-origin sealed/battery measurements in the same period (auditable against `factory/spawn_mining.py` `mine_sealed_trajectory()`); budget refusals recorded with the member and currency named. No serving number is claimed — the serving claim of any drill still goes through PL-005 and the battery (> 0.941, sealed ≥ 0.8611).
- **kill test:** Replay the recorded dream cycle (628 → 172) and cycles 004–007 under the budget rules: if the caps would have blocked the one measured dream WIN (dream-phaseC-002, confirm 0.8276 ≥ 0.80 bar) while passing the flat cycles, the budget punishes exactly the wrong spend — recalibrate once; if it still does, retire the caps and keep only the ledger.

### PL-005 ★ · The drill promotion ladder: dev-only until the battery speaks, in four rungs
- **source:** Cycles 6/7 are the standing proof that a mid-level gate is not a serving verdict: both children passed the pristine dual gates (phase-3 held + name-intent held, strict > parent) and still regressed serving (0.941 → 0.934 / 0.9375). - **mechanism:** Every drill gain — a retrained member, a refitted say, a new seat, a routing change — climbs the same four rungs, and only the fourth touches serving files. **Rung 1, dev win:** beats the incumbent on the drill's own dev exam; state = shadow, no file written. **Rung 2, fresh re-win:** beats it again on a newly generated pool (new seed, disjoint families) minted after rung 1 — a gain that only exists on its own exam dies here. **Rung 3, Gate 3′:** the full serving path (composer + waking-dream refresh) on the 216-item `say_dev` pool, child vs parent in memory — blocked on any served total fall or a ≥ 1-item served clean-recall fall, the exact cycle-6/7 signature `factory/growth_cycle.py`'s `_gate3_prime()` already enforces for births. **Rung 4, the battery speaks:** one battery run; promotion only if overall > 0.941, the candidate's own section ≥ incumbent, sealed ≥ 0.8611, audit clean — executed with the pre-drill byte snapshots `_snapshot_model_files()` takes, so a rung-4 failure restores bytes instead of deleting specialists (the cycle-006 repair's lesson, applied to drills).
- **change:** `factory/growth_cycle.py` — `_gate3_prime()` and `_snapshot_model_files()` / `_restore_model_files()` are reused as the ladder's rungs 3–4 machinery for drill candidates, not only cycle births; `factory/dream.py` — `run_dream_subject()`'s direct adoption block is replaced by ladder-state recording (rung reached, evidence per rung) in the run's `outcome.json`; `factory/learned_say.py` — `write_state()`'s adopted/shadow pattern is the state shape the ladder generalises.
- **judged by:** Replay first, and it is a hard bar: the recorded cycle-006 and cycle-007 winning children, re-run as drill candidates, must stop at or before rung 3 — both are known serving regressors. Live bar thereafter: zero ladder promotions followed by a battery regression, across the next 10 promotions; any promotion that regresses is reported as a ladder failure, not a candidate failure.
- **kill test:** The replay itself: if either historical regressor reaches rung 4, the ladder does not detect the known failure mode — do not ship it as a gate; keep drills dev-only permanently and say so.

### PL-006 · Member-vs-member round-robin: the 16 bug members drill each other's restraint
- **source:** The bug composition's members are individually strong and jointly weak: seat binary accuracies run 0.88–0.96 on dev (spawn-mining record) while the raw composed sealed accuracy is **0.4722** against the served path's 0.8611 — the loss is in how members fire on *each other's* classes, and every existing drill trains a member on its own positives. Mutable-default stands as the extreme case: a single seat, judge n=7 on the primary split, "near-anecdotal everywhere" (`factory/COMPOSITION-RESEARCH.md`).
- **mechanism:** A round-robin restraint drill: each seat's execution-proved positives are posed to every *other* seat as negatives, producing a 16×16 cross-fire matrix (seat × foreign class false-fire rate) on dev families. The drill diet for a seat is then built from its worst cross-fire cells — foreign positives it wrongly claims — paired with its own positives (PL-008's pairing), so practice buys *restraint* (not firing on a sibling's defect) rather than more recall on an already-strong binary. The matrix is re-measured after each round; a seat with no cross-fire cell above 0.05 sits the round out.
- **change:** `factory/dream.py` — `composed_eval()` gains the cross-fire pass over the injectable registry; `factory/compose_research.py` — `a1_preds()`'s harness reports the matrix beside its standing approach rows; drill diets flow through `run_dream_subject()`'s existing per-class retrain path, gates unchanged.
- **judged by:** Dev first: the worst cross-fire cell per drilled seat must fall, with that seat's own-class recall held (no recall-for-restraint trade beyond 0.01). Serving only via PL-005: Gate 3′ on `say_dev`, then the battery — raw composed sealed must rise above 0.4722's standing baseline *and* served sealed must beat 0.8611 with overall > 0.941; a restraint gain that only moves the raw number is reported as exactly that.
- **kill test:** The first matrix: if every off-diagonal cell is already ≤ 0.05 on dev, cross-fire is not the composed loss — the Phase D margin diagnosis (PL-002) stands unchallenged, and the round-robin is retired before any retraining spend.

### PL-007 · Dream-pool inventory: the pool as perishable stock, topped up on a rotation — not generated per crisis
- **source:** The dream supply is manufactured per cycle and mostly thrown away: 628 generated → 172 kept in the one full cycle on record, and only two niches have generators at all (`niche_generators()`: contract, commit). Between cycles there is no pool: a drill triggered tomorrow (PL-001) starts generation from zero, pays the full contamination tax again, and competes with the cycle that triggered it for the one heavy-job slot.
- **mechanism:** Pool-as-inventory, per niche and per class: a standing stock of execution-verified, contamination-filtered, *unused* items with two perishability rules — an item leaves stock when it has been trained on once, and stock older than a set number of regimen rounds is demoted to sparring fodder (PL-003) rather than diet, because a pool the members have effectively memorised through repeated screens stops measuring anything. A top-up leg on the daemon's cadence refills each weak class to its target stock (sized from PL-004's budget: e.g. 150–200 verified items per weak bug class), at the thermostat's current edge band, during idle time — so a drill draws stock instantly and generation spend is smoothed instead of spiked.
- **change:** `factory/dream.py` — `run_dream_subject()` draws diet/screen from inventory before generating, and `dream_state.json` gains the per-class inventory counts and ages; `factory/dream_gen.py` — `gen_pool()` gains the top-up entry point (target-count generation at a named band); `factory/daemon.py` — `process_one()` gains the top-up step under the governor.
- **judged by:** Supply bars, labelled as such: at every drill start in the next 10 drills, the target class's unused stock is > 0; top-up kept-rate (post-contamination) reported per rotation beside the 172/628 standing figure. Any candidate trained from stock reaches serving only via PL-005's ladder and the battery (> 0.941, sealed ≥ 0.8611) — inventory changes *when* items are made, never how gains are judged.
- **kill test:** Two consecutive top-up rotations whose post-contamination kept-rate falls under 10% — the generators are exhausting their own shape space against the contamination filter; expanding stock further buys duplicates, so retire the top-up and return to per-cycle generation with that recorded as the reason.

### PL-008 · Clean-sentinel paired drills: no defect drill without its clean twin in the room
- **source:** Clean is the hive's repeat victim, in three independent records: sealed clean recall **0.8182** (2 of the sealed slice's 5 misses at 31/36); the learned say lost exactly its 2 extra items on clean (0.8182 → 0.6364); cycle 006's regression was clean 0.8182 → 0.6364 and cycle 007's was 0.8182 → 0.7273, with defect classes standing still in both. A drill round whose paired score rises only by firing more on both twins scores zero. ### PL-009 · Spaced re-drill of mastered bands: what the thermostat steps past, the regimen steps back to
- **source:** The thermostat only moves forward: `factory/dream.py` sets `MASTERY = 0.90` and steps the edge band up when a band is mastered, and nothing ever re-probes a mastered band. The standing state shows the exposure: contract-judgment has band 1 `mastered` with edge band 2 (`dream_state.json`), and the bug classes show how non-monotonic this terrain is — off-by-one probe recall is 0.0 at band 1, 0.3571 at band 2, 0.0 at band 3 in the same sitting, so "mastered" at one band says little about a neighbouring one, and a mastered band that later rots is invisible until a battery section falls.
- **mechanism:** A spaced-repetition schedule over mastered bands: each mastered (class, band) is re-probed at expanding intervals (after 1, then 3, then 7 regimen rounds), on fresh draws only. A re-probe at ≥ 0.85 (mastery 0.90 minus a 0.05 grace) keeps the band mastered and doubles its interval; a re-probe below 0.85 un-masters the band, returns it to the drill queue at PL-001's priority, and resets its interval. No training happens on a re-probe — it is a measurement that schedules, never a diet.
- **change:** `factory/dream.py` — `Thermostat.measure()` gains the mastered-band re-probe schedule persisted in `dream_state.json`; `factory/dream_niches.py` — `_thermostat_entry()` / `_persist_thermostat()` gain the same schedule for niche subjects (contract's mastered band 1 is the first enrolled case).
- **judged by:** Coverage bar: 100% of mastered bands re-probed on schedule over the next 7 rounds, with every un-mastering followed by a drill within one cadence. Detection is the claim, not accuracy: any serving gain from a re-drill goes through PL-005 and the battery (> 0.941, no section regression) like every other drill gain.
- **kill test:** After 7 rounds, if no mastered band has ever dropped more than 0.02 on re-probe, decay at mastered bands is not occurring at this timescale — double the intervals once; if a further 7 rounds still show nothing, retire the re-probes and record mastered bands as stable.

### PL-011 · Drill-exam hygiene: one-shot drill exams, hash-registered, demoted to diet only after the next exam is minted
- **source:** The sealed v2 set has been measured in **11+ recorded trajectories** and now functions partly as a selection surface (H-013's source). The same disease is available one level down, unfiled: the 216-item `say_dev` pool is simultaneously the say's fitting pool, Gate 3′'s judging pool, and the obvious drill exam — a pool that fits, judges, and practices on the same items will report drill gains that are pool familiarity. Drill exams today have no hygiene discipline at all: no registry, no spend count, no retirement.
- **mechanism:** Drill exams get the sealed set's discipline at dev scale: each regimen round's exam is generated fresh, its item hashes registered before use, and it is spent *once* as that round's judge. Divergence between a candidate's spent-exam score and its fresh-exam score is reported as the regimen's own selection-pressure measurement.
- **change:** `factory/say_dev.py` — `load_dev_pool()` gains the exam-registry view (active exam vs demoted pools); `factory/executors.py` — `contamination_filter()` gains the active-exam reference set every drill diet is filtered against; `factory/dream.py` — `run_dream_subject()` records each round's exam hash and spend in its `outcome.json`.
- **judged by:** Audit bars: zero overlap between the active exam and any diet served in its round (the filter's own verdict, reported per round), and 100% of rounds carrying a registered exam hash. The pressure measurement is reported honestly either way: fresh-vs-spent divergence per candidate, beside its PL-005 rung. No serving number is claimed for hygiene itself; serving still requires the ladder and the battery (> 0.941, sealed ≥ 0.8611).
- **kill test:** Three consecutive rounds in which every candidate's fresh-exam and spent-exam scores land within one item of each other — at this pool scale the rotation is buying nothing measurable; retire the minting, keep the hash registry and the overlap check (those are cheap and catch real contamination).
