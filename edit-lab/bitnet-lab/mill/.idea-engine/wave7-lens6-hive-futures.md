### HF-001 ★ · The founding docket: niche founding as a routine pipeline, not an accident
- **source:** HV-011 is a post-mortem with a deadline bolted on: `data-format-model` sat unregistered through 70/70 gap answers because no process owned its birth — founding today happens only when a wave happens to include a niche (sec-code, sql-statement in expansion wave 1) or when a miner proposes one by hand (NX-005/006/007/009 are all hand-filed one-offs). At 10× the hive cannot found niches by essay; it needs an intake.
- **mechanism:** A standing docket with four stages and explicit pass criteria at each gate. **(1) Proposal** — a standard-form filing (demanded question shape, demand evidence count, whether labels can be proved by construction or by a checker, what the battery section would be) from any source: the gap sensor (HF-007), a miner, Arnav. **(2) Evidence audit** — demand evidence is re-derived from records, not trusted from the proposal (the HV-011 lesson: a niche nobody calls is not a niche). **(3) Feasibility** — a generator family + label-prover must exist on paper before any training spend; no prover, no founding. **(4) Probation** — the niche is built through the existing expansion machinery but serves *shadow-only* for one full battery cycle, then founds (registered, CLI verb per NX-001 parity, battery section per HV-004's pinned set) or is dissolved with the reason recorded in the docket. Dissolution is a normal outcome, not a failure state.
- **change:** New `factory/niche_expansion/docket.py` — proposal schema, stage transitions, and the docket ledger beside `expansion_cycle.py`; `expansion_cycle.py` gains a probation state between candidate and registered; founding writes the registry entry only on docket completion, so `factory/registry.py` `SPECIALIST_JOBS` is never touched by hand again.
- **judged by:** Process bars, labelled as such: every proposal reaches a stage verdict (founded / dissolved, with reason) within one expansion wave of its evidence audit; zero niches in the registry that did not pass through the docket; zero gap-answering job names (HV-011's limbo shape) older than one wave. Founding quality is judged by the existing expansion battery bars, unchanged.
- **kill test:** The first three proposals through the docket: if the pipeline founds a niche that then fails its own expansion battery bar in its first serving cycle, or dissolves two proposals whose demand evidence later proves real (gap calls for the same shape keep arriving), the intake criteria are mis-set in one direction or the other — stop, fix the criteria on those cases, do not let the docket run as ceremony.

### HF-002 ★ · Verdict federation: a read-only verdict-import protocol between the two hives on the repo
- **source:** Muse's side's hive and Fable's side's members are separate systems sharing one repo, and today the only cross-traffic is a human-carried candidate file (FABLE-CAND-03 arrived as text in `bitnet-lab/mill/FABLE-CANDIDATES.md` and was routed by a pool merge). Both sides independently measure overlapping question shapes (composition rules, JEPA, direct training) and each side's verdicts are invisible to the other's *machinery* — they arrive, if at all, as prose someone must re-derive. The valve permits exactly one direction: Muse's side may learn from Fable's side; it may never write to it.
- **mechanism:** A verdict record format and an import leg that lives entirely on Muse's side. A verdict record is evidence, not state: `(question-shape hash, verdict, method one-liner, evidence pointer, date)` — no model bytes, no training items, no sealed material, nothing executable. Muse's side's importer reads records Fable's side has *already published in its own normal course* (its lab notes / candidate files, as FABLE-CAND-03 was), normalises them into Muse's side's own `federation_inbox`, and files them as *claims to be checked*, never as facts: an imported verdict can spawn a local experiment (through the normal gates) but can never enter a training pool, a registry entry, or a battery directly. Nothing is written anywhere on Fable's side; if Fable's side publishes nothing in the format, the importer simply finds nothing — the protocol degrades to today's prose, it does not reach across.
- **change:** New protocol doc + importer on Muse's side only: `factory/federation.py` — `import_verdicts()` (parse → normalise → quarantine in `factory/federation_inbox.jsonl` with provenance) and `promote_to_experiment()` (the only exit from quarantine: creates a docket/experiment filing citing the imported verdict as source, judged locally from zero). No file on Fable's side is created, modified, or requested.
- **judged by:** Provenance bar: 100% of imported verdicts carry their Fable-side source pointer and sit in quarantine until a local experiment re-derives or refutes them; zero imported verdicts cited in any registry, battery, or training record as evidence in themselves. Value bar, reported after the first 10 imports: how many produced a local experiment that reached a verdict the local side did not already hold — the FABLE-CAND-03 precedent (one import, one live composition candidate) is the baseline shape.
- **kill test:** If the first 10 imported verdicts produce zero local experiments that reach a conclusion (all already-known, all untestable locally, or all too prose-bound to normalise), the format carries nothing the candidate files don't already carry by hand — retire the importer, keep hand-carried candidates as the federation mechanism, and record that verdicts do not travel lighter than prose at this scale.

### HF-003 · The disagreement ledger: cross-hive disagreements as a standing research signal
- **source:** The two hives have already disagreed productively once at scale: Fable's side's half-size control critique was *right* against Muse's side's layered-votes headline (plain 2.9010 vs layered 2.8990 — the headline was retracted), and both sides independently tested JEPA and both lost (token 2.894 vs JEPA 3.243 on Muse's side). Agreement and disagreement across the valve are both information, and today neither is recorded anywhere systematic — each instance is rediscovered from lab notes.
- **mechanism:** When HF-002's importer holds a Fable-side verdict on a question shape Muse's side has also measured, the pair is filed in a disagreement ledger with three states: *concordant* (both sides, same verdict — raises confidence cheaply, spends nothing), *discordant* (verdicts differ — the ledger entry becomes a docket filing for one local re-measurement under fresh conditions, because one of the two systems is wrong about a real question), and *one-sided* (only one side has measured — a coverage map of who knows what). Discordant entries are investigated, never averaged: the resolution is a local experiment's result, recorded against both original verdicts. Ledger entries never feed training (HF-008's firewall covers them as imports).
- **change:** `factory/federation.py` — `reconcile()` matches imported verdicts against Muse's side's own results records (battery evidence, lab RESULTS files) by question-shape hash and writes `factory/disagreement_ledger.jsonl`; discordant entries emit docket filings through HF-001's proposal shape.
- **judged by:** Resolution bar: every discordant entry reaches a recorded local resolution or an explicit "not locally testable" verdict; none is silently dropped. Calibration bar, reported per 10 entries: the concordant set's local re-measurement agreement rate — if concordant pairs routinely fail locally, cross-hive agreement is not evidence and the ledger's confidence reading is recalibrated to zero weight.
- **kill test:** The first 5 discordant entries: if local re-measurement resolves none of them (every one turns out to be a question-shape hash collision — the two sides were never answering the same question), shape-matching is too coarse to reconcile verdicts across systems; retire the ledger and keep HF-002's one-way import as filing material only.

### HF-004 ★ ⚠ · Standing staff charters: Arnav's recurring question streams routed to niches by default
- **source:** The hive passes Arnav's own tests unevenly: it is *usable by him* only when he remembers it exists — `serve_log.jsonl` (~500 lines) is overwhelmingly battery/growth-cycle self-calls, not a person asking questions (NX-011's source), while his real recurring question streams run elsewhere on standing schedules: the 8:06am Gmail triage with its VIP/needs-reply classes, the GeForce NOW morning sale watch, the Sunday Steam deal watch, the shared-memory secret-scan-on-write rule. NX-011's `try` verb fixes discoverability for ad-hoc questions; nothing makes the hive the *default first pass* for the streams that recur whether he remembers or not. ⚠ **Needs Arnav's ruling:** a charter makes the hive the default answerer for a real workflow of his; which streams get charters, and what each charter may decide without him, is his call per stream — this entry proposes the charter machinery and the first candidate list, and founds nothing on its own.
- **mechanism:** A charter per stream, in writing, with five fields: the stream (e.g. deal-watch claim text, shared-memory write payloads, triage sender+subject lines), the niche that answers (deal-claim NX-009, secret-line via NX-003 scan, inbox-triage NX-005 — charters name *existing or separately-founded* niches, they do not found niches themselves), the confidence floor (the niche's NX-002 abstention threshold — below it the stream's current human/agent path answers, untouched), what the hive's answer may *do* (label-only by default; acting on an answer is a separate charter line), and the review cadence (HF-010). A stream with no charter behaves exactly as today. The charter is the institution: default routing with a named owner, a named floor, and a named way back.
- **change:** New `factory/staff.py` — charter registry (`factory/charters.json`: stream, niche, floor, permitted effect, review cadence) and the routing shim the stream's existing runner calls before its current path; `hive.py` gains a `staff` verb that prints every active charter and its last review verdict, so the standing staff is inspectable in one command.
- **judged by:** Per charter, on its stream's own ground truth (the class the existing process actually assigned, the official page's actual sale state, the write's actual contents): the hive's above-floor answers must agree with the incumbent process at a pre-registered rate per charter (act-now-class agreement is the headline where the stream has one, per NX-005), and its below-floor abstention rate is reported, not hidden. A charter that cannot beat its stream's trivial baseline (keyword rule, date check) does not graduate from shadow logging to default.
- **kill test:** Per charter: two consecutive review periods (HF-010) in which above-floor answers disagree with the incumbent process on the costly direction (a missed act-now, a false official-live, a missed secret) more than the charter's pre-registered allowance — the charter is revoked automatically, the stream reverts to its pre-charter path the same day, and the revocation is the reported result, not a quiet downgrade to shadow.

### HF-005 · Succession protocol: no retirement takes effect until a named successor has shadow-matched the incumbent
- **source:** HV-002 can identify a dead-weight member (leave-one-out delta ≤ 0) and cycles 6/7 showed what an unmanaged transition costs (serving files deleted instead of restored; battery 0.941 → 0.934/0.9375; both repairs rebuilt specialists from nothing because no copy existed, REPAIR-cycle-006/007). The factory has birth gates and (after HV-006) a byte vault, but no *succession*: no rule that the seat is filled, by a measured successor, before the incumbent leaves it — at 80 niches, unmanaged exits are how coverage quietly develops holes like the data-format gap (70 calls, no specialist).
- **mechanism:** Every seat that serves (specialist job, composition class seat, substrate default) has a named successor-in-waiting once it is marked for retirement, distillation, or replacement: the successor is trained from the incumbent's own diet plus its recorded failure record (the per-class scorecards of HV-007), then runs shadow beside the incumbent on the dev pool and one battery cycle; the transition completes only when the successor holds the incumbent's battery section within 0.01 *and* matches or beats it on the incumbent's known failure shapes. Until then the incumbent serves — a slow succession is a delay, a skipped one is an outage. The retired incumbent then serves one further cycle as *emeritus*: callable in shadow only, so a late-surfacing succession regression has a byte-exact fallback that is still warm (with HV-006's vault as the cold store behind it).
- **change:** `factory/registry.py` `build_registry()` — entries gain `successor` and `emeritus_until` fields; `factory/growth.py` `attempt_birth()` gains the succession-birth kind (diet = incumbent diet + failure record); `factory/serve.py` `resolve_entry()` refuses to resolve a retired entry with no completed successor — the refusal is the protocol's teeth.
- **judged by:** Transition bar: zero serving gaps attributable to a transition (no job name answering "no specialist registered" because its incumbent left first) across the next 5 successions; each succession's shadow comparison recorded (successor vs incumbent per section and per failure shape). Serving battery must hold ≥ 0.941 through every transition, measured before and after as cycles already do.
- **kill test:** The first succession: if the successor cannot reach within 0.01 of the incumbent on the incumbent's own diet + failure record — the richest diet the factory can build for that seat — then incumbents are not replaceable by construction at this scale and every retirement is a coverage decision, not a transition; retire the protocol and require Arnav-visible sign-off per retirement instead.

### HF-006 · Distillation promotion: a saturated multi-seat niche graduates into one larger member, taught by its own composition
- **source:** Careers currently have one direction: more, smaller specialists (the decomposition principle — split any job that can split). Nothing covers the reverse move a 10× hive will need: the bug composition carries 16 members whose *composition* is the strong artifact (served 0.8611 vs raw 0.4722) while its seats individually run 0.88–0.96 binary — a niche whose seats have saturated and whose demand grows becomes a candidate to *consolidate*: one larger member (a bigger TinyNet / ternary model at LLM-adjacent width for that niche's shape) trained on the composition's execution-verified outputs. The factory already manufactures exactly the required diet: execution-proved labels at corpus scale (the dream machinery, 628 verified items in one cycle's record) with the composition + waking layer as the labelling teacher and execution — not the teacher's say-so — as label authority.
- **mechanism:** A graduation gate, not a default: a niche is eligible when (a) its seats' binary accuracies have plateaued across two drill regimens (PL-001's decay probes flat), (b) its served slice is carried by composition/verification rather than by any seat, and (c) call volume makes 16-member serving the latency bottleneck (NX-008's phase timings). The graduate trains on execution-verified items only — the teacher proposes items, the execution checker labels them, so the student can never learn the teacher's unverified errors (FABLE-CAND-03's same-method warning, applied to distillation). The composition is not deleted at graduation: it demotes to the graduate's fallback + emeritus (HF-005), and re-promotes automatically if the graduate regresses.
- **change:** `factory/dream.py` — a distillation run kind in `run_dream_subject()` (teacher = serving composition, diet = verified pool, student = single larger artifact registered as a new version of the niche); `factory/registry.py` `_apply_serving_rule()` extended so a graduate promotes only on the standing rule (measured battery slice ≥ the composition's), with the composition recorded as its fallback.
- **judged by:** The battery is the whole judgement: graduate's bug slice must hold ≥ 0.8611 with overall > 0.941, *and* raw (unverified-path) accuracy must beat the composition's raw 0.4722 — a graduate that only matches the served number by leaning on the same verification layer has consolidated nothing. Latency per call (NX-008 timings) must improve materially, or the consolidation bought nothing operational either.
- **kill test:** The first graduation attempt: if the student, trained on the full verified pool, cannot beat raw 0.4722 by a clear margin on dev, the composition's strength lives in the *diversity of the seats*, not in knowledge any single member can hold at this scale — retire distillation for this niche shape and record composition as the permanent form, not a larval stage.

### HF-007 · The gap ledger: niche discovery's sensor — every unanswered call tallies toward a founding proposal
- **source:** The hive already emits its own demand signal and discards it: `serve.py` answers unregistered jobs with a `gap` (data-format-model drew that answer 70/70 times — the loudest demand record in the log, found by hand, months later, in HV-011's mining) and classify niches answer `no-features` / (with NX-002) `low-margin` abstentions into the same void. H-014 mines the serve log for *growth targets inside existing niches*; no entry mines the log's *refusals* as the discovery surface for niches that don't exist yet.
- **mechanism:** A gap ledger, kept by the serving path itself: every serve response carrying a gap or abstention is tallied by (requested job or nearest niche, input shape hash family, day). When a gap family crosses a pre-registered threshold (≥ 20 calls, or calls on ≥ 3 distinct days — persistence, not one battery run's self-traffic; battery/growth caller tags from HV-003's log v2 are excluded so the hive cannot hallucinate demand from its own test traffic), the ledger auto-files an HF-001 docket proposal with the tally as its demand evidence. Below threshold, gaps are just counted — most gaps should never become niches, and the threshold is what keeps discovery a routine instead of a reflex.
- **change:** `factory/serve.py` `_append_serve_log()` — gap/abstention responses write a ledger line to `factory/gap_ledger.jsonl` (shape family + caller tag, no input content beyond what log v2 already keeps); `factory/spawn_mining.py` gains `mine_gaps()` emitting docket-ready proposals into `factory/niche_expansion/docket.py`'s intake (HF-001).
- **judged by:** Precision of the sensor, reported per quarter: of auto-filed proposals, the share whose evidence audit (HF-001 stage 2) confirms real external demand — target ≥ half; recall check against history: run over the existing log, the ledger must surface data-format-model as its top entry (70 calls is the known answer) — a sensor that misses the one proven case is not calibrated.
- **kill test:** The historical replay: if `mine_gaps()` over the current serve log ranks any battery-self-call artefact above data-format-model, the caller-tag exclusion has failed and the sensor measures the hive's own test traffic — fix the tagging or retire the sensor; do not tune the threshold to make the replay pass.

### HF-008 · The federation firewall: a mechanical import checker that makes contamination a failed check, not a promise
- **source:** HF-002/HF-003 open a channel from Fable's side's published record into Muse's side's machinery, and the lab's standing rule is that the valve is one-way and Fable's side is never contaminated — while the symmetric risk on Muse's side is quieter: imported material that overlaps Muse's side's *own* sealed/battery surfaces would poison the judges every gate trusts (H-013's sealed-v2 selection-pressure record shows how little reuse it takes), and imported text carrying instructions or payloads would ride in dressed as evidence. Promises in a protocol doc do not hold a boundary at 10×; a check does.
- **mechanism:** Every import passes one checker before it reaches the inbox: **(1) type check** — the record parses as a verdict record (HF-002's fields) and nothing else; free text beyond the method one-liner is stripped, so nothing instructional survives import. **(2) overlap check** — the record's question-shape hash and any quoted surface are checked against Muse's side's sealed sets, battery items, and held pools by the contamination filter's own rules (`factory/executors.py` `contamination_filter()`); any hit and the record is refused, with the refusal logged. **(3) provenance check** — the source pointer must resolve to a real location in Fable's side's published record as read at import time; unresolvable provenance is refused. Refusals are first-class records: the firewall's log is how the boundary's health is audited.
- **change:** `factory/federation.py` — `check_import()` runs all three checks before `import_verdicts()` may write; `factory/audit.py` `full_audit()` gains the federation section (imports total, refusals by check, zero unchecked imports as the pass bar).
- **judged by:** Audit bar: zero imports in `federation_inbox.jsonl` lacking a passing `check_import()` record, across every audit. Seeded test: a synthetic import batch containing one sealed-overlapping record, one unparseable record, and one record with a fabricated source pointer must be refused 3/3 by the named checks before the protocol's first real import is allowed.
- **kill test:** The seeded test itself: any one of the three planted bad records reaching the inbox retires the federation entries (HF-002/HF-003) until the checker is rebuilt — a firewall that fails its own drill is worse than no channel, because it certifies a boundary it does not hold.

### HF-009 · Sister hives: a meta-registry protocol for a second Muse-side hive that shares machinery, not members
- **source:** Multi-hive is usually read as the Fable question, but the nearer 10× step is on Muse's side itself: the factory's niches are all code-shaped, while NX-005/006/007/009 propose text niches (inbox, notes, messages, deal claims) with different data ethics (his mail shapes, his notes) and different failure costs. Cramming both families into one registry, one battery, and one serve path couples their risks — one hive's audit failure refuses both (HV-008's all-or-nothing load), and text-niche training material should never sit one config error away from code-niche pools. Fable's side is the existence proof that two hives can share a repo and machinery lineage while sharing no state.
- **mechanism:** A meta-registry above both: each Muse-side hive keeps its own registry, battery, sealed sets, and audit (byte-identical factory machinery, separate state directories), and the meta-layer holds only cross-hive *directory* information — which hive owns which job names, each hive's battery headline and audit status, and routing of a job name to its owning hive. Job names are namespaced (`code.*`, `text.*`) so no name can be claimed by both. Cross-hive learning uses HF-002's verdict protocol *between the sister hives too* — verdicts may cross, state never does — so the federation discipline is exercised daily at home, where failures are cheap, before it is trusted across the valve.
- **change:** New `factory/meta_registry.py` — the directory + routing layer over per-hive state dirs; `hive.py` resolves job names through it (single-hive behaviour byte-identical when only one hive is registered); the second hive's founding is an HF-001 docket act, not part of this entry — this entry builds the protocol it would be founded under.
- **judged by:** Isolation drills, in HV-008's game-day shape: corrupting the text hive's registry refuses the text hive only (code hive still serves at battery 0.941); a job-name collision attempt is refused by the meta-registry; a verdict crossing between sisters passes HF-008's firewall checks. Routing parity: every existing job name resolves to the same artifact as today.
- **kill test:** The isolation drill: if any single-hive fault (corrupt registry, failed audit, mid-growth state) degrades the other hive's serving in the drill, the separation is cosmetic — retire the meta-registry, keep one hive, and let the text niches be founded inside it under HF-001 with their coupling risk stated plainly.

### HF-010 · Charter review: the standing staff's error audit — wrong staff answers become drill targets, bad charters get revoked
- **source:** HF-004 ⚠ makes the hive a default answerer; a default without an audit is how a wrong niche becomes a wrong habit. The factory already knows how to audit *artifacts* (battery, audit.py) and how to drill *live failure shapes* (PL-012 drills fallback-unverified calls), but a staff answer is neither: it is a live answer, on Arnav's real stream, whose ground truth arrives later (the summary he actually sent, the official page's actual state) — and no entry closes that loop.
- **mechanism:** A weekly per-charter review with three moves. **(1) Score:** the week's above-floor staff answers are compared against the ground truth the incumbent process produced; per-charter error rate, split by direction (costly vs cheap errors, per the charter's own naming). **(2) Drill:** every costly error's shape becomes a drill target through the existing machinery (PL-012's shape-target path for composition niches, the niche dream cycle for classify niches) — staff errors get training priority over synthetic failure shapes, because they are proven-real shapes. **(3) Judge the charter:** error rate above the charter's pre-registered allowance in two consecutive reviews revokes it (HF-004's kill test, executed here as routine rather than as incident); a charter under its allowance renews automatically, and its floor may be *lowered* one step (more default, less fallback) only after two clean reviews — trust is earned in increments and lost in one step.
- **change:** `factory/staff.py` — `review_charters()` reads the week's staff-answer log (written by HF-004's routing shim: stream, niche, margin, answer, and the ground-truth field the stream's runner fills in later) and emits review records + drill targets; `factory/reporter.py` gains the staff section (per charter: answers, abstentions, error rate by direction, charter state).
- **judged by:** Loop-closure bars: 100% of costly staff errors appear as drill targets within one review cycle; 100% of charters reviewed on cadence (an unreviewed charter auto-suspends to shadow — the review is the licence); revocation and floor changes all traceable to review records. No serving-battery number is claimed; staff quality is judged on the streams' own ground truth.
- **kill test:** After the first 4 reviews across all charters: if ground truth is missing for more than half of staff answers (the streams' runners cannot practically fill it in), the audit is unfalsifiable and charters are running on trust — suspend all charters to shadow until the ground-truth feed exists; a staff that cannot be checked does not get to be a default.

### HF-011 · Career records: probation, tenure, and the per-member file that retirement and succession decisions read
- **source:** HV-007 mandates a per-class before/after scorecard on every *state-changing event*; nothing keeps the per-*member* longitudinal record those events belong to. The factory's decisions about members are already career decisions made without a file: cycle 006/007's children were adopted on two pristine slices with their remainder collapse (0.4444 vs parent 1.0000) logged as "informational"; the learned say sits in indefinite shadow with no stated path or deadline; members born in Phase 3 have no probation concept distinguishing them from members with six cycles of clean service. At 16 members this is memory work; at 160 it is impossible.
- **mechanism:** One career file per member/seat, appended at every event the member participates in (birth with its gate scores, drills with rung reached per PL-005, battery sections it contributed to, incidents — regressions it caused or suffered, successions it was party to). Two decision rules read the file. **Probation:** a member's first two battery cycles after birth are probationary — its section is reported separately in the battery record, and a probationary regression triggers HF-005 succession review immediately rather than at the next audit. **Tenure:** a member with ≥ 4 consecutive clean cycles and a positive leave-one-out delta (HV-002) at its last audit is tenured — it can still be retired, but only via full succession (HF-005), never by a single cycle's gate result; tenure is protection from churn, not from evidence. The say's shadow state becomes a career state too: shadow members carry a review date by which they must reach rung 4 (PL-005) or be dissolved back to a research file.
- **change:** `factory/registry.py` — career file per entry (`factory/careers/<entry>.jsonl`) written by the same adoption helper HV-006 routes all state changes through; `factory/growth_cycle.py` battery reporting splits probationary sections; `factory/audit.py` `full_audit()` fails a tenured entry retired without a completed succession record.
- **judged by:** Record bars: 100% of serving members have career files whose event count matches the lineage/scorecard records (HV-006/HV-007 cross-check); the cycle-006/007 children, replayed through the career rules, must show probationary status at adoption and an immediate succession review on regression — the two historical cases the file exists to catch. Decision bar thereafter: zero tenured retirements without succession, zero probationary regressions unreviewed.
- **kill test:** If reconstructing career files for the current 16 composition members + serving specialists from existing records leaves more than a third of their known events (births, the cycle incidents, the say's shadow history) unrecoverable, the file starts too late to govern the current hive — ship it prospectively only, say so in the record, and do not let tenure/probation rules read a file known to be partial.

### HF-012 ⚠ · A joint cross-hive exam: one fresh question set, both hives answer, verdicts compared
- **source:** HF-003's disagreement ledger can only reconcile verdicts on questions both sides *happened* to measure — overlap so far is accidental (composition rules, JEPA, direct training). The calibration both sides actually lack is a *designed* overlap: the same fresh, never-trained question set answered by Muse's side's hive and by Fable's side's members in the same period, so agreement rates are measured on identical material instead of inferred from neighbouring experiments. ⚠ **Needs Arnav's ruling, unavoidably:** this asks Fable's side to *do* something — run an exam it did not schedule, in a format set across the valve. That is a write across the one-way valve in everything but file bytes, and it also spends Fable's side's compute on Muse's side's question. Filed so the option exists with its price named; HF-002/HF-003 are the valve-respecting default and lose little if this is never approved.
- **mechanism:** (If ruled in.) A small exam (36–60 items, the sealed-v2 size class) built by Muse's side from new template families, decontaminated against *both* sides' published training surfaces as far as they are visible, published as a plain question file (questions only — no Muse-side answers, no sealed material, nothing from any battery). Fable's side answers it in its own course and publishes its answers in its own record; HF-002's importer brings the verdicts home through HF-008's firewall; HF-003's ledger reconciles.
