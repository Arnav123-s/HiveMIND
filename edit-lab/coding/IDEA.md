# The Idea — solidified 2026-11-08 (Arnav's corrections; building follows THIS, not the earlier patches)

1. **The unit is the smallest possible specialist MODEL.** A tiny learned model that does exactly one smallest job. Not a generalist. Not a rule-script ("calculator") — a model. It is made good at its job by training OR by direct weight editing via mapping. Its right size is the smallest size at which it does its one job perfectly.

2. **Specialists live in niches, in layers.** A niche is one small corner of a field. Every niche holds only its own micro-specialists plus its own micro-coordinator — coordination is itself a smallest specialist job. No general planners, no general coordinators, at any level. The structure is fractal: niches inside niches.

3. **The brain is the hive.** There is no central model. Intelligence is a property of the whole swarm working together — shared board, niche coordinators, clones and voting, lessons socialized across the hive. Bigger and harder tasks are solved by COMPOSITION: more specialists cooperating. Never by enlarging a specialist into a generalist.

4. **As many as the device holds.** The target is the maximum number of smallest specialists running at once inside the machine constraint (7 GB here). The constraint is the proof ground: if it runs within the limit here, it runs smooth anywhere.

5. **Growth instead of refusal.** When the swarm meets a problem it cannot do, standing meta-specialists respond: harvesters find and gather data, trainers train new smallest specialists, surgeons map and edit weights directly, learners learn from failures, spawners create more specialists for coverage or for the new problem. An unknown problem triggers growth of the missing piece — verified, then socialized to the whole hive.

6. **Surgery follows the protocol.** Any weight or architecture change: trace the existing model, find every responsible weight, make the map, untangle the whole model, then change only what the map indicts. No change before the map exists.

7. **Operation: end to end, unattended, precise.** The swarm takes Arnav's instructions and works every task to completion with no human in the loop, following the instruction precisely — compliance is checked against the literal instruction, item by item.

8. **Development: trainer, optimizer, surgeon — until they run alone.** For now Muse develops the specialists slowly in those three roles. Graduation is autonomy: the swarm completing tasks end to end, growing through gaps, with no optimizer intervening.

9. **Field order: coding first.** The swarm targets coding as a whole field — every niche of it — and proves itself there. Other fields come after coding works.

10. **Proof standard.** A specialist, a niche, a capability counts only as far as it is verified — probes, held-out checks, the win rule against the incumbent. Scope is claimed only as much as is proved.

11. **Frontier models are a tool, not the brain.** Through an OpenAI/Claude-style interface, a specialist may call a frontier model as an escalation — but the mind is the hive itself, local and self-sufficient by design.

*Why (the through-line of Arnav's work): one mind made more capable by being systematic — many small verified pieces, aware of failure, improving from it. The climb, at swarm scale.*

## Addendum — Arnav, 2026-11-08 13:30 PT
- Composition failure is solvable like any failure: decompose further and/or compose differently; it is never a dead end.
- DREAMING: specialists practice offline on many examples; dreams improve internals/weights directly, and dream traces make the surgeon's maps more precise — better indictment, more precise edits.
- MULTIPLE SPECIALISTS PER TASK: the same micro-task may have several variant specialists, each slightly better at a part of it; both contribute, routed/combined by measured sub-strengths. The smaller the specialization in the smallest completable task, the better.
- EVERYTHING BY SPECIALISTS (Arnav, 2026-11-08 13:31 PT): the swarm's own operations are specialist jobs too — decomposition itself has decomposer specialists; training, surgery, harvesting, dreaming, spawning, coordinating likewise. Fractal all the way down: any function that DOES a job is a smallest specialist; only the harness/gates (the measuring instruments) are code.
