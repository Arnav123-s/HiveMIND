# Same-work scheduling replay

Preserve each scoreable fresh and fixed probe and both arms. Train duration estimates on older generations alone. Estimate an unseen fresh job from the median older duration for its easy/hard prefix; a fixed job uses the median from its own prior fixed identifier. Prior measurements pool both arms. Predict the fixed, per-seat overhead from the previous generation for the before/after role and seed, subtracting archived task seconds from full runner time. This residual accounts for setup, smoke, talk, upload and idle time as a single non-movable block. Clamp a negative prediction to zero. Sort tasks in decreasing estimated duration, breaking ties by identifier, and assign the next task to the least predicted-loaded runner in its own arm; keep eight runners per arm, preserve fresh-before-held phase on each runner, and leave all extra work including talk on the original host. The replay uses actual held-out measured costs only *after* placement. G52 is the primary calibration/test. G51 is an extra test if G50 job timings can be recovered. Simple controls are the original placement, round robin, uniform-overhead longest-estimate-first, and a hindsight schedule charged each seat's actual residual. The maximum of average load and original immovable residuals forms a necessary lower bound.
