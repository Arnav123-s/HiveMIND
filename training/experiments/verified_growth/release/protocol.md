# Fixed-exam audit and frozen entrant comparison

The endpoint is the repository's complete 30-task fixed held-out exam, three jobs for each of ten kinds. A kind passes with two of three correct. Report task and kind scores, availability of recorded workers, and how many distinct kinds retained two successes under each counterfactual. The historical baseline uses the G52 after-body for the direct paired comparison. Also report the retained G51 after-body and both arms of G50–52 to distinguish generation-specific variation from a change in capability.

Rebuild a compact, keyed ledger from all 48 existing work archives with their published SHA-256 digests. Confirm that all six arms present the same 30 prompt hashes. Distinguish delivered passes, any recorded member passes, and their union across archives. A missing member verdict is unknown. An upper bound on selecting a recorded answer supplies no upper bound on composing, repairing or generating a new one. Keep the public fixed exam and its keys out of training and into the evaluator only.

Score the externally developed, frozen composer on the 30 exact user messages as delivered by the official runner, with the same available user input files. Supply no task ID, family, answer hints, file synthesized by the examiner, or historical output. Keep its raw receipts before invoking any exam checker. For any delivered answer, run the original question's checker on the actual report text and files. Count abstentions as zero for the entrant alone. In the paired release replay, keep the original G52 answer on abstention. If a claimed answer fails, the candidate loses the point even if the original passed. This comparison does not regenerate archived workers. Repeated exposure to a static public exam limits external validity.

Record the two wrong G52 pulse deliveries identified in the prior public composition audit and compare the attribution of the even-number and continuously compounded interest examples with the source records. No reported pulse job is mixed into the fixed-exam denominator. This protocol and harness are isolated from the composer's development.
