# Frozen pulse replay protocol

Use every fresh mixed pulse shard from both arms of generations 50, 51 and 52. Generation 50 is the engineering split. The release comparison uses generations 51 and 52 with exact duplicates of engineering or earlier evaluation prompts removed. Do not use held-exam tasks embedded in the same archives, practice data, or live web/website, file delivery, generated module, or repair tasks. Include deterministic text checks from m_days, m_weekday, m_percent, m_units, m_know, m_follow, m_steps, m_table, m_translate, m_summary, h_search, h_sim, h_since, h_two, h_txt_ini, h_txt_words, h_txt_access, h_txt_contacts and h_txt_sentences where the checker requires no mutable external source or file. Recreate the task from the seed, accept only an identical recorded prompt. Describe exclusions and missing seats against the entire fresh pulse.

Predeclare the real, counterfactual four-seat panel in order: after-arm members 1 and 2, then before-arm members 1 and 2 on the exact same request. An explicitly returned empty answer is an observed nonvote. A missing seat is censored. Use the complete cohort for paired policy comparisons, report its coverage and counts on the full audience separately. Do not claim that four workers ran in one original session, or that the added cells were newly generated. They ran independently in two natural recorded arms. The four seats can share models and training.

The selector sees only released outputs in order. Collapse case, whitespace and punctuation at the ends. Treat uncomplicated equivalent numeric literals (optional currency sign, thousands separators, decimal spelling and sentence-final punctuation) as the same value without rounding. Do not extract arbitrary numbers from prose. A class releases its earliest actual answer; every release requires at least two votes. The fixed-four reference takes the most votes, resolving a tie by its earliest seat. The rateless policy stops as soon as no possible assignment of the remaining seats can change that delivery, including silence or a fresh answer. It asks between two and four seats. Freeze these rules before opening evaluation cases or reading their outcomes.

Controls include fixed two and three seats, fixed four with ties withheld, and a four-seat analogue of main's reserve-on-crash with the same answer grouping. Report archived actual after-body performance separately. Main uses richer task-aware agreement and delivery checks and can now add two seats after an initial three. Show why these comparisons answer different questions. The gate observes no type/level, model identity, grade, future answer, or timing. Independently apply the regenerated exam checker to the selected original output only; verify it independently against recorded worker verdicts. Timing is the sum of archived sequential seat intervals for the seats purchased, conditional on reliable timestamps. Do not call that a measured live speedup.

Report correct, wrong, withheld, coverage, wrong per delivery, seats bought, count early exits, fixed-four equivalence and failures shared by the four-seat rules. Publish selection receipts, source identities and deterministic reproduction, including split and component checksums. No rule tuning after opening either evaluation generation. No new model generation is needed if the real paired archives can be recovered.
