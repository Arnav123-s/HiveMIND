# Twelve mechanisms against the HiveMIND record

The released body in the audited main revision was G51. It gained one paired delivery (49/64 → 50/64) and went from 1/30 to 3/30 on the fixed held-out exam. G52 tied 52/64, fell from 2/30 to 1/30 on that exam, and was dropped. Two of its 64 tasks had a correct member and an incorrect final delivery. These figures establish both a selection problem and a harder problem of producing any correct candidate. The changing pulse cohorts make 50 → 52 an invalid estimate of generation gain. [G51](../../../docs/reads/g51.md) · [G52](../../../docs/reads/g52.md) · [Paired decision code](../../grow_record.py)

“Main” below refers to `4cbd41c` and “PR 111” to `70d0f3b`. Prior offline experiments, ordinary runtime features and proposed tests are identified separately. Each proposed comparison uses independently keyed tasks and counts actual delivered answers, abstentions and compute. Published source mechanisms supply hypotheses. Their reported speed, accuracy or convergence cannot be transferred to this implementation.

The four suggested bundles cover six mechanisms because the veto pairs with diversity and selection pairs with migration. Testing each component alone and together exposes which one earned any gain. A positive microbenchmark warrants a paired G51-body trial on new requests before changing the release gate.

Trace setup pays for itself once cumulative savings exceed recording cost, guard misses and fallbacks. If two workers have equal error rate `p` and binary error correlation `r`, their joint failure probability is `p² + r·p·(1−p)`. Both failing can still produce two different wrong answers. With uniform starting prices, an LMSR market maker risks at most `b·ln(n)` in subsidy for `n` mutually exclusive outcomes and liquidity parameter `b`. Answer accuracy needs its own measurement. [Trace guards](https://www.cs.williams.edu/~freund/cs434/gal-trace.pdf) · [Correlated failures](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSE.1986.6312924) · [Market model](https://hanson.gmu.edu/mktscore.pdf)

### Which limits actually bind?

- **Memory traffic.** A 2.03 GiB weight file is about 2.18 GB. Under an assumed full uncached read per serial target step, bandwidth divided by bytes read bounds those steps per second. File size alone gives no universal bound on delivered tokens or correct jobs per second. Measure memory traffic, batching, draft verification, and all overhead on the actual hardware. [Measured CPU setup](../../reports/rule_lab/continual-learning-and-speed.md)
- **Ambiguous intent.** Identical visible evidence can support incompatible specifications. Additional generated tests can expose disagreement. Resolving it requires a distinguishing observation, authoritative convention or clarification. If both meanings yield the same answer on the requested inputs, delivery can still be safe. [Real program ambiguity](../../reports/verified-learning/experiment/challenge.md)
- **Rank 16.** Rank bounds the dimension of an effective linear update to one matrix. A target update of higher rank cannot be represented exactly by a single such edit. Distinct skills may share directions, and routed edits need not fit together. In this sample rank 4, 12 and 16 tied on the scored tokens. [Rank ablation](merging/README.md)
- **Closed-world self-play.** Unanchored recursive training can lose rare outcomes. A simulator or exact checker can still establish genuinely new consequences of supplied rules. Repeating the same imperfect model cannot establish missing facts about an unobserved external world. Hold out externally sourced cases and track rare capabilities over time. The cited 2025 article is a position paper qualifying collapse claims. [Recursive-data experiment, 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07566-y) · [Position paper, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03150)
