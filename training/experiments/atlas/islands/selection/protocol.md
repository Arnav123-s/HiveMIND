# Fixed-budget subset selection control

This follow-on reuses the frozen island protocol and previously examined 30-case public test panel. Freeze the selectors before measuring this comparison. The training pool, 18 exact domain-by-answer quotas, 30 selected rows, three class-ordered rounds, two pull settings (0.5 primary, 0.1 sensitivity), rank-four optimizer, migration and evaluator are unchanged. All selector computations below use the same 60 teaching rows alone. No test label, prediction or test state enters the selectors.

* **Random half.** Within each stratum take the half with smallest SHA-256 of seed, a separator and row ID. Use all five prespecified seeds 23, 29, 31, 37, 41. Report every outcome and their mean/range.
* **Hard half.** Run the original selector unchanged (largest base error margin). Check selected IDs and recovered case predictions against the existing receipts.
* **Market and its frozen-price control.** Build a market with 60 mutually exclusive outcomes, one latent demand for each teaching item. This is a research allocation mechanism; the actual outcomes are not separately settled. Normalize training vectors to unit length, subtract their training-set mean and normalize again. For each example form a unit bundle consisting of 0.5 shares on itself and 0.25 on each of its two closest other training vectors by cosine (ties by row ID). Let U be within-stratum error-margin mid-percentile (largest margin gets largest percentile); let R be 1/(1 + number of inbound neighbor endorsements). Value is 1 + U + R. R measures local representation rarity; the fixed quotas already control class counts.
* Start with all outstanding claims q=0 and fixed liquidity b=0.5. The LMSR cost is C(q)=b log(sum exp(q/b)); marginal outcome prices p are softmax(q/b). Among all examples still eligible under their quota choose the largest value divided by its current bundle marginal price p·bundle, breaking ties by row ID. Execute exactly one bundle, debit the exact finite-trade cost C(q+bundle)−C(q), update q and repeat 30 times. Budget of 15 cash units covers even the worst possible total cost of 30 such bundles (each component is at most 0.5). Log trades, marginal quotes, executed costs, cash, nearest neighbors and closing prices. Static control uses 1/60 for every marginal price throughout, preserving the same values, bundles and quotations at the first decision.
* Report after-migration oracle-domain local and single-trunk scores, 18 earlier / 12 supplementary split, per-case changes, CPU and diversity/coverage of the selected rows. Pre-registered estimands are market minus static (price effect on this corpus) and market/hard minus random for each pull. Pre-migration outcomes are secondary. Repeat selection plus training five times with rotated order after warm-up, single-thread process CPU. No tuning, behavioral rollout or production changes.
