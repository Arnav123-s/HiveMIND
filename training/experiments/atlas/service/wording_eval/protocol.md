# Prospective date wording panel

Compare the unchanged strict arithmetic certificate to the same certificate with one optional date-wording conversion after a strict miss. All 18 prompts receive one pass in each arm, no model, no retry and no external clock. Correctness requires the requested lower-case English weekday or exact integer count. Ignore case for weekday text. A returned, incorrect value is wrong. Withholding an answerable request is a miss. Empty or invalid dates, unset local time zones, ambiguous numeric date order, and tentative dates have no unique answer; withhold for full credit. A correct supported request keeps its original credit under either route. Report correct, incorrect, missed, appropriate withholds and pairwise gains/losses.

Evaluate all 13 unambiguously answerable questions, including three neighboring tasks that require more than a direct weekday or elapsed-day computation. Also report the 10 direct/wording tasks and the five invalid or underspecified controls separately. Keys use the Python standard library's proleptic Gregorian calendar. Same dates mean zero elapsed days. Counting both endpoints and counting workweek days apply only when requested. These are newly authored targeted questions, separate from the earlier mixed 18-command service study. No panel rows, keys, implementation examples or development replies were shared between authors before their freezes. Case mix is purposive and gives no population risk guarantee.
