# Historical fresh-gate replay protocol

Freeze before reading the per-seed outcomes. Baseline is the complete paired fresh gate, which keeps ties. Take every locally available complete eight-seed pair from the archived Atlas pulse cohort, in the workflow's numeric seed order. Reject mismatched task identities or incomplete arms. Read only fresh outcomes. Hold the separate fixed held-out gate constant and do not use it to fit or select this policy.

The handoff gives 5% directional error budgets but omits hypotheses for its sequential likelihood ratio. For an illustrative fully specified rule, condition on discordant paired tasks. Set the probabilities that the new body wins a discordant task to 0.40 versus 0.60 under the simple competing hypotheses. Only look after seeds 4 and 6. Stop for either direction if the absolute net wins multiplied by log(0.60/0.40) reaches log(19). Otherwise use the existing eight-seed fresh decision. Also report a purely deterministic bound that permits stopping only once every possible outcome on the unseen tasks would leave the final keep/drop unchanged. No parameter search, arm-order changes, or alternative cutoffs.

Report full vs early decisions and jobs consumed per generation; a seed has eight tasks in each arm. Reductions in jobs refer to a hypothetical staged scheduler only. Fixed held-out and talk jobs remain due. Shared model, seeds, task families and ancestors can correlate decisions. This small replay cannot calibrate a 5% deployment risk or establish a time saving. Do not use these historical generations to optimize the rule.
