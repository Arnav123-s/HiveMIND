# Chronological minority-switch audit

Freeze before grading this study. Use all available original fresh G50–G56 gate-work archives, both arms, all eight seed seats. Read no fixed exam files. G50–52 are development and G53–56 evaluation. Use after bodies as the primary series; tabulate before separately. The two arms share prompts and generations share lineage. The analyst has previously seen aggregate/two-arm scores and some historical examples. These are retrospective gates, not newly blind test questions.

A proposed switch concerns a real, already delivered answer. It requires exactly three observable worker answers, exactly one agreeing pair among them under HiveMIND's own printed-answer comparison, and a delivered printed answer supporting the pair, not the singleton. Distinguish text-only from files and part-by-part work. Truncated or missing answers, missing reports, file-content-only agreement, and overlapping/nontransitive agreements are ineligible. Do not solicit a fourth worker or fit on before/after repeats. Retrospective worker grades can reveal an opportunity only after a choice is made. Report losses and gains even where correct voters agree on the wrong interface; do not create a counterfactual artifact from worker grades.

Compare stock against two rules. The first may switch a text-only response if the singleton meets an explicit user-given output contract and both majority answers violate the same contract. Restrict contracts to exactly N nonempty lines and explicit JSON-only output; do not infer a task answer or use hidden verifiers. The second additionally requires better runtime history (fewer failed program executions, then fewer hints; ties leave stock alone). Calibrate permission for this extra gate only on development after-arm switches: at least five switches and at least 80% observed precision, otherwise return stock on evaluation. Report an ungated runtime-history ranker as a negative control, which switches text-only proposals strictly better than both voting peers. Never use a hidden grade, the wording of a hidden checker, or the eventual selection as a predictor of worker quality. Keep rules frozen even on zero qualifying cases.

For true observed split opportunities with per-member grades report an oracle bound, including cases with requested files as a separate retrospective bound. Stock failures with any right worker form a looser ceiling including unavailable answers, abstentions and insufficient voters. Ideal switch gains require a wrong incumbent and a correct minority; an available word from the wrong program is not proof of a file. Report all denominators, correct, spoiled, wrong-to-wrong, and both-correct changes. Flip precision denotes the fraction of all measured switches where wrong delivery became right; give exact binomial confidence limits as descriptive only. Report no precision when there were no switches, no production modification, and no official held-out credit.
