# Chronological, top-one specialist selection

Freeze before collecting practice outcomes or scoring the chooser. Fit only on G50–52 practice work archives (the original twelve practice shards per generation), using the user's own request words and individually graded worker receipts. Restrict to unambiguous single requests with exactly one recorded correct worker among at least two distinct, identified, checked and completed workers. Require a complete mapping between runtime completed seats and individual verdicts. Agreement or a selected training row is insufficient. A win is unique among the workers who actually attempted that practice job, not the whole hive. Deduplicate identical normalized practice request text and remove competing unique winners for the same text.

Build a unit-length TF-IDF vector for each unique practice request, using alphabetic words from that request only, log term frequency, IDF = 1 + ln((1 + document count) / (1 + document frequency)), and cosine similarity. The prototype of each named actor is the normalized sum of its vectors. Choose one actor, at unit selection weight, among the first two actors advertised in the initial seats event before any worker runs. Both are scheduled without depending on a third-seat tie break. On ties, absent actor prototypes, zero evidence, missing roster, or the same actor in both slots, defer to the first seat. Never use grades, worker text, task ID, kind, lineage hints from the exam, later seat count or a future roster as inputs. This experiment replays existing worker choices; new weight attachment and new generations are outside its scope.

G50 development gates use G50 practice, G51 uses G50+G51, and G52 uses all three prior-to-gate practice waves. G53–56 are the chronological evaluation: freeze that final practice index throughout. Fit no hyperparameters or thresholds on the gates and read no fixed/held exam content. Compare against first seat, second seat unconditionally, and greater historical unique-win count (same unknown/tie fallback). Report after as primary and before separately. Exclude from evaluation any normalized exact request text (case-folded with whitespace collapsed) shared with G50–52 fresh gates in either arm or any G50–52 fitting practice job. Development similarly excludes exact overlap with its practice index. Structural similarity between independently seeded tasks can remain.

Compute choices before opening official job/worker grades, then score only observed, correctly matched seat outcomes. Report missing and excluded rows, new/unseen actors and coverage. On a missing second observation no right/wrong result is invented for that actor. Report the common fully matched cohort beside the complete first-seat observations, both-available cases, flips, gains and spoiled answers, and compare candidate availability to official delivery. A previously counted leaked prompt or an unseated actor cannot earn a transfer gain. A perfect-choice bound uses only actual known actor-specific grades. Candidate methods here reproduce selection order, not unobserved consequences of moving a second worker's recorded behavior into the first seat.
