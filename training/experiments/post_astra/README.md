# What improved in HiveMIND

On the same 30 unseen requests the original G55 hive delivered three correct answers. Asking workers to inspect unfamiliar inputs and check the public requirements raised that to four. One task kind reached the pass threshold. The separately trained model scored two. The five-kind target remains open.

| Official G55 comparison | Correct deliveries | Kinds passed | Requests with a correct worker answer |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original hive | 3/30 | 0/10 | 9/30 |
| Workers inspect unfamiliar input and check public requirements | 4/30 | 1/10 | 6/30 |
| Four new training updates applied to the shared first worker | 2/30 | 0/10 | 5/30 |

Each kind has three requests and needs two correct deliveries. All 24 exam jobs completed and all 90 outcomes were graded. The inspection change gained two CSV edits and one date-filtering task, and lost a ranking and a two-source task. It delivered all three CSV edits. The fitted body gained two individual tasks and lost three. These are whole-hive scores over one paired exam. The ordinary production line independently recorded G57 at 49/64 on its own pulse and 1/10 on its fixed exam. ## Using an earlier skill on a new job

On four fresh tasks a worker reused a reader learned on an earlier file-processing job. The reader decoded INI records. The worker wrote the new calculation for each label's range. Offering the appropriate reader raised complete solutions from **1/4 to 2/4** and individual checks from **33/96 to 62/96**. Removing the reader from the winning program dropped its score from 24/24 to 0/24. In an offline replay using the same programs, the public input alone selected the correct reader on all four jobs and the selector abstained on all five incompatible or ambiguous controls. Plain text matching picked the INI reader even on CSV inputs and scored 1/4. This comparison covers two verified readers. [Source programs, controls and timings](../hint_dream/README.md)

An ordinary local chat can now call an earlier worker's verified function before loading a model. Three archived functions qualified across all 12,041 inputs in their declared ranges. An independently checked zero input rejected a fourth worker that had passed its original practice task. With the bank and WASI runtime configured, the command below returns **16** and identifies the worker and the 1,000 checks behind this particular method. The review branch also supports a persistent bank shared from practice to subsequent local, web and hosted chat. [Setup and reproduction](../astra_transfer/bank_admission/README.md)

```sh
./hive --local --once 'Calculate the decimal digit sum of 907.'
```

## What the five tickets established

- **CPU time and memory.** On two archived training examples, the 128-token loss chunks and per-row memory fallback lowered peak memory from 12.17 to 10.48 GiB. The full weight update matched bit for bit. Time rose from 205 to 234 seconds. The sequential gate saved zero jobs over nine full historical generations. The owner account cancelled the 20-job timing run after 2h20m46s. The logs prove at least 255 optimizer updates, but no adapter reached the gate. The complete generation time and final memory-skip total are unknown. [Native comparison](../astra_cpu/measurement.md) · [Historical scheduling](../astra_cpu/gate_timing/README.md) · [Hosted receipt](../hosted_generation/README.md)
- **Dropped training examples.** The 1,152-token cutoff excluded 157 distinct examples across 81 requests. Nine requests lost every example. Two genuine programs were just three tokens over the limit. At 1,155 tokens the guarded CPU trainer processed both intact, completed an update and exported its adapter in 6m38s, at 10.83 GiB peak with zero skips. Their effect on accuracy has not been measured. [Twenty hosted filters and full-row native run](../hosted_generation/lengths/README.md)
- **Choosing workers.** The prespecified two-worker setting delivered 4/6 on the original panel. An exploratory change to the second worker, with a third available, reached 5/6 in CPython and WASI. On six fresh requests the original and revised settings each delivered 3/6 with zero wrong. One fewer call raised summed inference time from 940 to 1,034 seconds. Six historical lookup pairs were withheld because public evidence was insufficient. [Original and fresh results](https://github.com/Arnav123-s/HiveMIND/tree/a5e6c02/training/experiments/astra_orders/replication)
- **Recovering an interrupted answer.** In a local three-turn CSV case the worker wrote the right file but the token limit cut it off before it could print the count. Allowing the next reply to spend the remaining tokens produced the correct whole delivery in 422 tokens, down from 576. On three fresh tasks neither arm passed and none encountered that failure condition. The hosted inspection exam used the earlier fixed settings. [Exact graded recovery](https://github.com/Arnav123-s/HiveMIND/tree/23b20d3/training/experiments/astra_orders/worker_contracts/continue_budget) · [Three prospective controls](https://github.com/Arnav123-s/HiveMIND/tree/9fc8e88/training/experiments/astra_orders/worker_contracts/prospective)
- **Learning and keeping old skills.** A private hint produced a verified correction on 1/6 initial tasks and 3/5 follow-up tasks, for 4/11 overall. Four CPU updates taught the model to reproduce two corrected programs unaided. Both passed 64 fresh inputs apiece. On three different tasks it improved from 9/72 to 12/72 checks, with 0/3 whole tasks solved by either model. Three separate native proposals trained with two new examples per old example. All preserved both old checks, tied the incumbent at 3/5 and were rejected. Three matched fresh-only proposals gave the same 15 whole-task verdicts. The differing example came last at the lowest learning rate. Three full improving generations and a score above 58/64 remain unproven.
