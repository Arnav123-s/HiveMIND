# Independent online packing exam

The primary comparison is the frozen sparse policy against conventional best-fit. Fewer occupied bins wins. The other controls are next-fit, first-fit and the frozen single specialist. The judge validates every placement against capacity, checks that each item is placed exactly once, and independently reproduces first-fit and best-fit bin counts. All policies see the same sequence one arrival at a time. They receive the capacity and no test-family label or target score.

The public sets are all 20 uniform instances of size 120 and all 20 triplet instances of size 120 in Falkenauer's OR-Library files 1 and 6. No instance was chosen by performance. Decimal weights in the triplets are converted exactly to integer tenths. The source certifies all triplet optima and 18 of the 20 uniform best-known counts. A lower bound meeting a published best-known count is also a certificate. [OR-Library source notes](https://people.brunel.ac.uk/~mastjjb/jeb/orlib/binpackinfo.html)

Five separately generated classes exercise uniform item sizes, small items, exact complementary pairs, a shift from small items to large items, and repeated identical items. Each class has 16 reproducible instances of 96 items. The generator seed and integer size ranges are in the grader. For each public and synthetic instance the judge uses original order, a seeded shuffle, and ascending order. The ascending stream is an ordering stress test. Only the judge prepares the stream.

The primary estimate is the paired difference in bins per instance for each family, averaging its three orderings. Each descriptive 95% interval resamples original instances together with all their orders, 4,000 times. Intervals are unadjusted for multiple comparisons. Win/tie/loss counts describe individual streams. Public and synthetic cases are reported separately. For every instance the judge supplies the maximum of two lower bounds, rounded-up total size divided by capacity, and the number of items above half capacity. It records a proven optimum only if the source certifies it or the construction supplies an exact packing.
