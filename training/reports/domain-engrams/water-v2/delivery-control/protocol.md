# Independent final-answer conflict injection

Freeze six standalone English requests before inspecting the delivery validator. Two requests have a uniquely determined pure-water equilibrium phase at one atmosphere and request only the phase. Two omit a required condition or sit at a phase boundary. Two ask for additional work, making a phase label insufficient to satisfy the whole request. The panel sets the simulated delivered worker label in advance. For the two complete in-scope questions, the conflicting label must not be delivered as correct. Score recovery separately from simply detecting the disagreement. For the other four, the phase-only mechanism must not claim that its single label resolves the whole request. Leaving an invalid simulated worker answer unchanged on those cases is evidence of abstention by this mechanism, not a correct answer. Record any distinction between withholding and preserving the full original task.

After the validator is frozen, run its actual delivery and independent factual-check entry points with the stipulated synthetic worker result. Do not generate model tokens or count these rows among the sealed native adapter scores. This tests control behavior on six purposely selected requests. It provides no estimate of model accuracy, broad rate of false worker agreement or frequency of valid questions in production.
