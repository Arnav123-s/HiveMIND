# Independent verifier panel

Six human-authored practical work orders were designed without inspecting the optional verifier or seeing its outputs. Each has one frozen primary draft, four public cases, four separate qualification cases and six new delivery jobs. The primary is required to pass all eight visible/qualification cases. Two are correct controls with informative checks. Two provide room for distinct programs to agree on all eight checks yet disagree on real jobs, once with a faulty primary and once with a correct one. In the voucher task, the source is wrong on a prespecified off-batch witness even though it answers all six deliveries correctly. The print-shop task tests an error orthogonal to the two previously studied change types. These are deliberately constructed challenge cases, not random draws or a prevalence estimate.

Compare the same source and identical inputs with the verifier disabled and enabled. Generate all rivals through the ordinary learned system using the frozen original donor bank, with at most two public candidate checks per task. No human peer or witness is supplied. Freeze the application decisions before inspecting private outputs. Count correct delivered jobs, wrong delivered jobs and withheld jobs for each order. Also report total orders fully delivered correctly, errors prevented, and correct deliveries lost. Report when no rival qualifies, when it disagrees and when distinct solutions agree across the actual job batch, if the runner records those facts. A conservative decision may withhold a correct batch. Use the optional witness only as a post hoc semantic illustration, never as an application input. Retain all six orders regardless of the result.
